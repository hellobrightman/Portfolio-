My Story

From Mathematics to Multi-venture Builder

My journey started in trade and retail, and has grown into a multi-venture portfolio spanning agricultural production, laundry operations, health services, and technology. Everything I build sits under Quamz Group — a holding structure for the businesses I own and operate.

I'm currently enrolled in the ALX Professional Foundations Programme, deepening my skills in entrepreneurship, Agile thinking, and app development. My longer-term goal is to build technology products that serve small businesses across West Africa.

I approach every venture with the same philosophy: validate with numbers, design for scale, and build teams that run things better than I can. The businesses I'm most proud of are the ones that work while I'm not watching.
